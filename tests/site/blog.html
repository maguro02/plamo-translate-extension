Releasing Example Framework 3.0

We spent the last year rewriting the build pipeline. It's a no-brainer: if you don't pin your dependencies, your builds will eventually break. That is why 3.0 now generates a lockfile by default.

The new release ships with a handful of breaking changes, so read the migration guide before upgrading. Most projects need only a few small edits.

Under the hood, the scheduler batches requests to keep the GPU busy. This alone cut our inference costs by a third.

Streaming responses are also available. This feature is opt-in and off by default.

We'd love to hear your feedback, so drop us a line on GitHub. Thank you for using Example Framework.
